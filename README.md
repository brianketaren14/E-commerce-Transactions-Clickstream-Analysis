# E-commerce Transactions + Clickstream Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes **e-commerce transaction** and **clickstream** data to evaluate sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, and website conversion efficiency. By combining transactional data with user interaction events, the analysis provides business insights into product performance, revenue generation, profitability, and customer conversion across the purchasing funnel.

The project includes:

- 📦 Sales volume analysis
- 💰 Revenue analysis
- 📈 Profit analysis
- 💵 Product margin analysis
- 🛒 Customer conversion funnel analysis

# Funnel Analysis

| Conversion | Rate |
|------------|-----:|
| View → Cart | 26.54% |

| View → Purchase | 10.95% |

<img width="1189" height="590" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/70c42b56-d8b3-487f-a992-516f20f56203"/>

### Insights

- The overall funnel achieved a **10.95% View-to-Purchase conversion rate**.
- The largest drop-off occurred between **Page View → Add to Cart**, where approximately **73%** of visitors left without adding a product to their cart.
- More than half of customers who added items to their cart proceeded to checkout, indicating relatively strong purchase intent.
- The **Checkout → Purchase** stage achieved the highest conversion rate (**74.93%**), suggesting that the checkout and payment processes are effective.

---

# Key Findings

- The **best-selling product**, **highest-revenue product**, and **most profitable product** are different, indicating that sales volume alone does not determine business value.
- **Lipstick LightBlue 766** demonstrates consistent demand and stable monthly sales, making it a reliable volume driver.
- **Mouse RosyBrown 419** is the strongest revenue generator but exhibits greater revenue volatility.
- **Headphones Aquamarine 465** delivers the highest profit despite inconsistent sales, reflecting its higher profit per unit.
- **Doll LightSkyBlue 279** has exceptional margin but weak sales performance, making it a potential candidate for promotional campaigns.
- Since the greatest customer drop-off occurs before products are added to the cart, improving product pages, pricing strategy, product recommendations, and website usability is likely to produce the largest increase in conversion and overall sales.
